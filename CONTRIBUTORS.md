## Contributing to the research

This project recognises contributions to problem formulation, organisational practice, data stewardship, measurement, critical review, experimental design, software, documentation, and replication. Contributions do not have to be code, and expertise in JEPA is not a prerequisite.

Contributors may add their preferred name, public profile, and a short factual description of their contribution here through a pull request. Attribution is opt-in. Do not identify a pilot participant, employer, customer, or research partner without their permission. Git history provides the record of code and document changes; it does not determine authorship of future papers.

## Authorship and assistance

Authorship and acknowledgements for future publications will be agreed on the basis of actual contributions before submission. Repository participation does not imply endorsement of every claim or experiment.

AI tools may assist drafting, coding, and review. Human contributors remain responsible for the resulting work, citations, claims, and tests. The initial repository scaffold was prepared with AI assistance and executable checks; this is not a substitute for independent scientific review. Do not list tools as human authors or imply that generated text is empirical evidence.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to participate.
