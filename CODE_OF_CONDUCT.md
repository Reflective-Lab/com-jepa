# Code of conduct

We want rigorous disagreement and a setting where people can contribute without being demeaned or exposed. Treat organisational practitioners, researchers, developers, and newcomers with equal respect.

- Critique claims, evidence, and methods. Explain objections so others can examine them.
- Welcome uncertainty, corrections, negative results, and different professional perspectives.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or make personal attacks.
- Do not reveal another person's identity, private communications, organisational records, or participation without permission.
- Do not misrepresent authorship, affiliations, evidence, or experimental findings.

These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-organised sessions. The maintainer may request changes, remove harmful content, restrict participation, or exclude repeated or serious violations. Moderation should be proportionate and protect affected participants' privacy.

Report sensitive conduct concerns through the maintainer's public contact channels at [Reflective](https://www.reflective.se), asking for a private route before sharing details. Do not post identifying allegations or confidential evidence in public issues. The private security-reporting route is also appropriate when a concern involves data exposure.
